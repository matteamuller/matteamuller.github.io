About the research

Profile / Research focus

I am a researcher working at the intersection of nutrition science, microbiology, bioinformatics and clinical medicine — driven by the question of how individual dietary patterns shape metabolic health through the gut microbiome and metabolome.

My long-term goal is to establish an independent research group focused on precision nutrition and multi-omics approaches, developing personalized strategies that translate complex biological data into actionable public-health insights. I combine extensive experience in human intervention trials with computational expertise in microbiome analysis, metabolomics, and causal machine learning.

I trained as a cell biologist (Osnabrück), molecular life scientist (HU Berlin), and earned my PhD at Maastricht University on dietary-fiber modulation of gastrointestinal transit and metabolic health. I now combine that wet-lab foundation with a Master's in Biomedical Data Science (MHH) to drive translational multi-omics research.

Current projects

Active & upcoming research
Active · PI

Computational Precision Nutrition

CPN-MAP · BMFTR Junior Research Group

Building an integrative computational framework that combines diet, gut microbiome and metabolome data to predict individual metabolic outcomes. Leveraging causal machine learning and multi-cohort data (LifeLines DEEP, TREND, FoCus, PERSON, Estonian Biobank, TwinsUK) to uncover biological mechanisms linking dietary patterns to health.

5 years · 1.48M € PLRI · TU BS / MHH
Active · PI

FAIR4CPN

NFDI4Health Use Case · Dietary Index Harmonization

Calculating and harmonizing widely used dietary indices across three large European health studies (LifeLines, TwinsUK, Estonian Biobank) and an MHH lifestyle intervention. Documenting scoring algorithms in a machine-readable form to make nutritional epidemiology reproducible and FAIR.

30 months NFDI4Health
Applied · PI

CED Navigator

IBD · Patient-Centered Nutritional Framework

Creating a patient-centered framework for translating nutritional evidence into routine IBD care. Addressing the gap between strong scientific evidence on dietary fiber and the fragmented, often restrictive eating patterns adopted by patients with Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis. In collaboration with Dr. Miriam Wiestler, MHH.

Clinical Translation w/ MHH Gastroenterology
DGEM 2025

Multiomics in iRBD & Parkinson's

Diet · Microbiome · Metabolome — Early Markers

Investigating the interplay between diet, microbiome and metabolome in REM Sleep Behaviour Disorder as early markers of Parkinson's disease. Shotgun metagenomics and untargeted metabolomics on PASS-PD and FoCus cohort samples — a multi-omics approach to neurodegenerative risk.

DGEM Forschungsförderung 15 months
2022–2025

NutriPredict

Automated ML for Dietary Health Prediction

Investigating the use of automated machine learning to predict the health benefits of dietary modifications. Funded by the Joachim Herz Stiftung Add-On Fellowship for Interdisciplinary Life Science.

3 years Joachim Herz Stiftung
