Mattea Müller

Junior Research Group Leader · Computational Precision Nutrition
Peter L. Reichertz Institute for Medical Informatics, MHH & TU Braunschweig

I lead the Computational Precision Nutrition (CPN) group, working at the intersection of nutrition science, microbiome research, bioinformatics and clinical medicine. My research maps how individual dietary patterns shape metabolic health through the gut microbiome and metabolome, using multi-omics integration and causal machine learning.

I trained as a cell biologist (Osnabrück) and molecular life scientist (HU Berlin), earned my PhD at Maastricht University on dietary-fiber modulation of metabolic health, and completed an M.Sc. in Biomedical Data Science at MHH.

My long-term goal is to establish an independent research group developing personalized strategies that translate complex multi-omics data into actionable public-health insights.

projects selected research activities

PI · 2025–2030

Computational Precision Nutrition

CPN-Map — BMFTR Junior Research Group

An integrative computational framework combining diet, gut microbiome and metabolome data to predict individual metabolic outcomes. Leverages causal machine learning across multi-cohort data (LifeLines DEEP, TREND, FoCus, PERSON, Estonian Biobank, TwinsUK) to uncover biological mechanisms linking dietary patterns to health.

PLRI · TU Braunschweig & MHH · 5 years
PI · 2026–2028

FAIR4CPN

NFDI4Health Use Case — Dietary Index Harmonization

Harmonizing widely used dietary indices across three large European cohorts (LifeLines, TwinsUK, Estonian Biobank) and a lifestyle intervention study. Documenting scoring algorithms in a machine-readable form to make nutritional epidemiology reproducible and FAIR-compliant.

NFDI4Health · 30 months
PI · 2026–2028

CED Navigator

IBD — Patient-Centered Nutritional Framework

A patient-centered framework for translating nutritional evidence into routine IBD care, addressing the gap between scientific evidence on dietary fiber and the fragmented, often restrictive eating patterns adopted by patients with Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis.

w/ Dr. Miriam Wiestler, MHH Gastroenterology
PI · 2025–2026

Multiomics in iRBD & Parkinson's

Diet · Microbiome · Metabolome — Early Markers

Investigating the interplay between diet, microbiome and metabolome in REM Sleep Behaviour Disorder as early markers of Parkinson's disease. Shotgun metagenomics and untargeted metabolomics on PASS-PD and FoCus cohort samples.

DGEM Forschungsförderung · 15 months
PI · 2022–2025

Automated ML for Dietary Health Prediction

Using automated machine learning to predict the health benefits of dietary modifications. Funded through the Add-On Fellowship for Interdisciplinary Life Science.
